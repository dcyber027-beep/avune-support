Avune Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

Avune records, edits and mixes audio on your iPhone. The app has no account system, advertising, tracking or in-app analytics. Recording, importing and editing in Avune do not send your audio or projects to the developer.

Microphone permission

Avune requests permission when you start a microphone recording. It uses the microphone while you record a take. You can decline and still import, edit and export audio or try the built-in demos. You can change the permission in iPhone Settings under Microphone.

Audio and projects on your iPhone

When you save a take or import audio, Avune keeps a local copy of the source and the information needed to reopen it, such as its name, length and edit settings. Studio tracks keep their own local copies. Avune creates temporary audio files for playback and export. Your iPhone’s backup settings determine whether app data is included in an Apple device backup.

Delete a recording or Studio project from its options menu. A Studio project can still contain audio from a recording you deleted from the library. To remove the audio held by the project, delete that project. To remove all app data from this iPhone, choose Delete App in iPhone Settings. Manage any device backup or copies in Files separately.

Importing, exporting and sharing

The Files picker gives Avune access to the audio you select. When you share a WAV, the iOS share sheet passes it to the destination you choose. File providers, destinations and Apple’s services handle those copies under their own policies. Avune has no built-in sync service.

This website

Avune opens the Support and Privacy Policy links in your browser. This site uses no analytics scripts, advertising or forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you send. It is used to respond and follow up on your request. Support correspondence may be kept as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. You can ask about your email or request deletion by writing to zhenghaolou@outlook.com.
