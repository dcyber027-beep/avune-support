Avune Support

Get help with Avune for iPhone. Avune supports iOS 15 and later.

Contact support

Email zuckerloushh@outlook.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to the issue. Start with a description of the problem and use a built-in demo to reproduce it when possible.

Record or import audio

Open Recordings and choose Record or Import Audio. If you can’t record, check Avune’s microphone permission in iPhone Settings. If an import fails, make sure the mono or stereo audio file opens in Files and is available to download from its provider.

Try the editor

In Recordings, scroll to Demo Recordings and open Voice with rumble. Switch between Original and Improved while you listen. Open Fine-tune for more controls or use Trim to keep the part you need.

Export a WAV

In the recording editor, tap Export, choose a version, then tap Share WAV. In Studio, tap Mix / Export, then Export Mix. Choose Save to Files for a local copy, or choose a destination from the iOS share sheet.

Manage your recordings

Use the options menu next to a recording or Studio project to delete it. Studio tracks have their own audio copies. To remove all copies inside a Studio project, delete that project. Manage copies in Files or other apps through the destination that holds them.
